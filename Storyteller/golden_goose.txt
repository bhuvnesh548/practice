Once there was a poor farmer who lived in a small village. Although he worked hard every day, he did not earn much money.
The farmer owned a small farm and a few animals. Among them was a goose.
One morning, while collecting eggs, the farmer noticed something unusual.
Inside the goose's nest was a beautiful golden egg.
The farmer could not believe his eyes.
"Is this really gold?" he wondered.
He took the egg to the village and showed it to a goldsmith.
The goldsmith examined it carefully and said, "Yes, this is pure gold."
The farmer was amazed.
He sold the golden egg and earned more money than he had ever seen before.
The next morning, the goose laid another golden egg.
And the morning after that, another one.
Every day, the farmer became a little richer.
At first, he was happy and grateful. He repaired his house, bought better food, and lived comfortably.
But as time passed, greed entered his heart.
"Why should I wait for one egg every day?" he thought. "There must be many golden eggs inside the goose."
The farmer became impatient.
One morning, he made a terrible decision.
He decided to kill the goose and take all the golden eggs at once.
But when he opened the goose, he found nothing.
There were no golden eggs inside.
The farmer had destroyed the very thing that had made him rich.
Now he would receive no more golden eggs.
He sat beside his empty farm and realized what he had done.
"If only I had been patient," he said sadly. "I had everything I needed, but I wanted more."
The farmer remained poor, and he spent the rest of his life remembering the mistake caused by his greed.
the end.

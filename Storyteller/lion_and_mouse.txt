Once upon a time, deep inside a vast and beautiful forest, there lived a powerful lion. He was known as the king of the animals. Every creature in the forest respected him, and many were afraid of his great strength and loud roar.
One warm afternoon, the lion had eaten a large meal and was resting under a tall tree. Soon, he fell into a deep sleep.
Nearby, a small mouse was playing with his friends. While running through the grass, the little mouse accidentally climbed onto the lion's huge paw.
The lion suddenly woke up.
With one quick movement, he caught the mouse under his massive paw.
The mouse was terrified. He trembled and begged, "Please forgive me, Your Majesty. I did not mean to disturb you. Please let me go, and perhaps one day I will be able to help you."
The lion looked at the tiny mouse and began to laugh.
"You?" he said. "How could such a small creature ever help someone as powerful as me?"
But the mouse continued to beg, and the lion was in a good mood. Finally, he lifted his paw and allowed the mouse to run away.
Several days passed.
One morning, the lion was walking through another part of the forest when he became trapped in a hunter's strong net. He pulled and roared with all his strength, but the ropes only became tighter.
The animals heard his roar but were too frightened to come near.
Then the little mouse heard the sound.
He immediately remembered the lion's kindness.
The mouse followed the sound and found the lion trapped in the net.
"Do not worry, Your Majesty," said the mouse. "I will help you."
The lion could hardly believe what he was hearing.
The little mouse quickly began chewing through the ropes with his sharp teeth. He worked for a long time, cutting one rope after another.
Finally, the net broke.
The lion stepped out and was free.
He looked down at the small mouse and said, "I was wrong to laugh at you. You may be small, but you have a great heart. Today, you saved my life."
The mouse smiled.
From that day onward, the lion and the mouse became friends.
the end.

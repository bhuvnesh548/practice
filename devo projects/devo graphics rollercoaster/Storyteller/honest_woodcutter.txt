Once upon a time, there was a poor woodcutter who lived in a small village with his family.
Every morning, he went into the forest with his old iron axe.
He cut branches and collected wood, which he later sold in the village.
Although he did not earn much money, the woodcutter was honest and hardworking.
One day, he was cutting wood beside a deep river.
As he swung his axe, his hands slipped.
The axe fell from his hands and disappeared into the water.
The woodcutter looked into the river, but the water was too deep and dark.
He could not see the axe.
"Oh no!" he cried. "That axe is my only tool. Without it, how will I earn money for my family?"
The woodcutter sat beside the river and began to cry.
Suddenly, the surface of the water began to shine.
A mysterious spirit appeared from the river.
"Why are you crying?" she asked.
The woodcutter explained what had happened.
The spirit disappeared beneath the water.
A few moments later, she returned holding a beautiful golden axe.
"Is this your axe?" she asked.
The woodcutter looked at the golden axe.
It was worth more money than he had ever seen.
But he shook his head.
"No," he said. "That is not mine."
The spirit disappeared again.
This time, she returned holding a shining silver axe.
"Is this yours?" she asked.
Again, the woodcutter shook his head.
"No. That is also not mine."
The spirit disappeared one final time.
When she returned, she was holding the woodcutter's old iron axe.
The woodcutter's face immediately filled with joy.
"Yes!" he cried. "That is my axe! Thank you!"
The spirit smiled.
"You were honest even when you had the opportunity to become rich," she said.
As a reward, she gave the woodcutter the golden axe and the silver axe as well.
The woodcutter returned home with gratitude.
His family was amazed by his good fortune.
From that day onward, he lived comfortably, but he never forgot the importance of honesty.
the end.
